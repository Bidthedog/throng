# The throng user guide

throng is a desktop workspace for people who live in the command line. Each project gets its own
folder, colour and layout of terminals, editors and previews, and throng puts it all back as
you left it. Terminals keep running when you close the window, and throng tells you when one of them
needs you.

**New to throng? Start with the [Quick start](quick-start.md).** It takes you from first launch to a
working project in about ten minutes.

What throng does, and where to read about it:

- **[Projects](projects.md)**: one folder, one colour and one saved layout per piece of work, switched in a click.
- **[Tabs, panels and layout](layout.md)**: split, drag and stack terminals, editors and previews however you work.
- **[Sub-workspaces](sub-workspaces.md)**: tear tabs and panels off into windows of their own, across screens.
- **[Terminals](terminals.md)**: every shell on your system, kept running behind the window and reattached with their scrollback.
- **[Knowing when something needs you](attention.md)**: marks, notifications and a jump to the next panel waiting for you.
- **[Files](files.md)**: a file tree that moves, copies, undoes and works with your OS's file manager.
- **[Editing](editing.md)**: an editor with syntax colouring for 55 languages, multiple cursors and crash recovery.
- **[Finding things](finding.md)**: Quick Open, find and replace, and search across a whole project.
- **[Previews](previews.md)**: live previews beside the file you are editing, and printing to paper or PDF.
- **[Markdown](markdown.md)**, **[CSV and spreadsheets](csv-and-spreadsheets.md)** and
  **[images, PDFs, SVG and HTML](images-pdfs-svg-html.md)**: what each preview can do.
- **[Preferences](preferences.md)**, **[themes and icons](themes-and-icons.md)** and
  **[key bindings](key-bindings.md)**: make throng look and behave the way you want.
- **[Watching resources](resources.md)**: see what throng and everything it started is using, and stop what runs away.

When something goes wrong, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md); for short answers, the [FAQ](faq.md).
Every setting is described in the [preferences reference](../../docs/preferences.md), every theme setting in the
[theme settings reference](../../docs/theme-settings.md), and every shortcut in the
[key bindings reference](../../docs/key-bindings.md).

## Get help from inside throng

**Anywhere (F1), or cog menu › Help**

The guide is one key away while you work.

1. Press **F1**, or open the cog menu at the top right and choose **Help**. This page opens in throng's Help
   window, and works offline: it is the guide that ships with your copy of throng.
2. Click the **?** beside any setting, key binding or theme setting, or anywhere else in throng, to open its own
   section in the same window.
3. Use **Back** and **Forward** (or **Alt+Left** and **Alt+Right**) to step between pages, and **Open in browser**
   to read the same page on throng-ide.com.

> [!NOTE]
> In a terminal, F1 goes to the program running there. Use the cog menu instead.
